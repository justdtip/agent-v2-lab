What the slope is made of

The vocabulary is 248,320 tokens wide, so by arithmetic the bf16 logits, their fp32 log-softmax and the logit gradient would cost 1.89 MiB per token, three-quarters of the 2.48 MiB per token that preflight's calibration measured for the no-recurrence floor. That arithmetic was the hypothesis behind variant B, and the measurement refutes it: the two variants differ by 0.24 MiB per token, about a tenth of the slope, and their step times are identical. MLX never holds the whole logit block, its log-softmax and its gradient at once, so the loss was never where the memory went.

Removing the full logits cuts the slope from 2.40 to 2.16 MiB per token and moves the working-set ceiling from about 6,404 to about 7,257 tokens. Roughly 2 MiB per token of row remains unexplained by the logits, the layer-boundary residuals (0.16 MiB) or the attention scores (quadratic, and too small below 4k). The candidates are gradient checkpointing not holding in MLX's lazy graph, so that many layers' activations are alive at once, or the recurrence retaining per-token tensors. That is the next probe: the same step with checkpointing off, and with the recurrence's backward removed, up to the recipe's own row length.

Method

Each point is its own process: the registry spec resolved through evaluate.load_policy, which takes the model-run lock; LoRA rank 16, scale 32 on the resolved keys over every layer; the chunkwise gated-delta recurrence at chunk 64; gradient checkpointing on; AdamW; mlx_lm's default_loss on a synthetic row, batch 1. Two steps run at each length and the second is reported. Variant B runs the backbone once and applies the vocabulary projection and cross-entropy per 1,024-position chunk under mx.checkpoint, so the full logits are never resident; it reproduces variant A's loss to three decimals. Lengths stop where the linear projection from the last two points exceeds 85 percent of the working set, and a guard terminates a step on two consecutive critical kernel-pressure samples. Efficiency probes stop at 64k context by the Director's ruling (R48); this one never approached it.
