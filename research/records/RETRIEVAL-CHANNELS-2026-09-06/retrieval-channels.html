Does the query move with the question?

Median cosine between a head's read queries: two retrieval questions on the same context, two no-lookup controls, and across contexts. The retrieval questions share a template and the controls do not, so this contrast measures wording variety, not retrieval intent: a corpus limitation, not a result. What it does show is that the attention query turns barely more than the recurrent one and retrieves anyway.

recurrentattention
The mechanism is the read, not the query. Attention's read is a softmax over positions, a competition in which a small margin in the dot product becomes a large margin in mass, so a nearly static query still selects the answer. The recurrent read is a linear sum over a superposed store: at 1,500 positions the one matching key contributes about eleven times a non-matching one and there are 1,499 non-matching ones, so the crosstalk swamps the signal by two orders of magnitude and the span share lands at uniform. Same store, different reader: the classical capacity result for linear associative memories, about one item per dimension, reproduced in a production model. The three weak key-head candidates from the ledger run did not declare themselves here (best ratios 1.8 to 2.1, ranks 478 to 952 of 13,479) and that item closes.

Method

Attention blocks are named by the repository's block index and the layer they write (block 19 writes layer 20). Recurrent heads with gate constants under 200 tokens were not measured; 256 to 299 of 768 were, per context. All 48 retrieval answers were correct, so whether concentration tracks correctness is untested here. Eight seeded contexts of four kinds (sixteen key-value needles, reference-back prose with a two-hop form, code constants, in-context learning of a symbol-to-digit mapping), facts early and questions at the end with at least 300 tokens between, six retrieval questions and three no-lookup controls each, every question its own forward through the 4-bit checkpoint. Recurrent read weights are |alpha| by the backward scan at the read position for heads whose gate constant on the context exceeds 200 tokens; attention weights are the softmax recomputed from the post-rotary queries and keys and checked by reconstructing each block's own output (gate 0.05 of the output scale, proven to fail on a wrong head pairing). Both channels go through one statistic: the share of far-source (gap at least 257) mass on the answer's token span, against the uniform share and against 50 random unit queries at the real norm. Controls use a placebo span from the context's own facts. The model's answers are scored under teacher forcing over the whole answer.
