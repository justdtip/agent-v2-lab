How to read it

Each column is one token the model actually wrote, in order. Each row is a layer, with the first layer at the bottom and the last at the top. A cell's colour is how much probability the lens, reading the residual at that layer and position, gives to the token the model went on to write. The row of marks above the map shows the first layer at which the lens ranks that token first. Hover or tap a cell to see the lens's own top guess there.
