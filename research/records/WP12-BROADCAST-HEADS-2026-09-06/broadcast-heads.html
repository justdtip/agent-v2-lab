Stage 2: ablating the relays, graded, on the five-layer band

Where the thread stands, with the weight of each leg. Established: stage 1 finds a real and reproducible population of relays by weight arithmetic, and single-head ablation shows no member of it is individually special (a proper within-layer null). Open: at set level the relays damage the readout more than random sets and do so redundantly, and that is consistent with both readings, specific carried content (heads that damage the same content carry the same content, which is what broadcast looks like from outside) and general influence; the injection arm built to separate them did not achieve sensitivity at its pre-registered readout. Generated, not tested: a reversal at the output-aligned readout, p = 0.10. The paper's causal claim is therefore not supported at single-head resolution and undecided at set level. The contrast injection at layer 20 then showed that the same-position readout cannot test broadcast at all, because the residual stream carries injected content past any ablated head; the discriminating experiment is the paper's injected-thought report protocol, filed for the next use of the model.
The metric. Each arm is scored by the overlap of the lens readout's top-25 tokens with the unablated readout's own top-25, so the unablated row is 1.0 by construction and the number is damage to the model's prior output, not recall of anything external. The paper's recall at 25 scored the lens's recovery of an externally known target and starts at 0.86; it is not the same quantity and the two are not compared. The paper's question is whether a small specific set does the work of a much larger arbitrary one, so each selected arm is also expressed as the number of random heads at which the random curve reaches the same overlap, interpolated linearly in log2(k + 1) between the two measured random arms that bracket it, which are shown beside every figure because the transform does real work.

What the set-level curve does and does not show, after the single-head sweep below. The set-level effect stands as measured: the top relays zeroed together damage the readout more than layer-matched random sets, and the sets are strongly redundant (measured overlap above the product of the single-head overlaps at every k). But it is not lens-specific transport by individually special heads: relays are level with the unselected heads at every layer when ablated alone, the small-k advantage is one strong layer-12 head counted at all ten readouts, and the concentration factor is arithmetic of the layer mix. Random ablation of any two heads already costs ten points and the selection adds six. The cumulative factor falling with k is not a validated ranking: per head, ranks three to eight add half of what ranks nine to sixteen add, and the sweep found no rank correlation between preservation and single-head effect. Whether redundancy means the relays carry the same workspace content, or that they are not special, is what the injection test decides. Specificity: at k = 21 the selected set changes the next token at 0.178 of positions against the random sets' 0.118; breaking the model would move the output far more than a control that damages the readout less.

The in-band relays under the orthogonalised rule, 26 heads writing layers 12, 16, 20, 24 and 28, are zeroed in order of absolute lens preservation, k at a time, and the lens is read at all ten band layers on eight 512-token documents at 64 positions each. Beside each arm, five layer-matched random sets of the same size drawn from the unselected heads at the same layers. The layer-4 relays do not couple to the band (the Director's hypothesis, causal test)

Each of the sixteen heads writing layer 4 zeroed alone; per head, the change it causes in the 26 in-band relays' own attention outputs and in the 54 unselected in-band heads'. The three layer-4 relays change the relays' outputs by a median of 0.072 against 0.058 for the other thirteen heads (one-sided p = 0.12), and their selectivity, the change caused in the relays over the change caused in the unselected heads, is 0.89, 0.77, 0.91 against 0.73 to 0.92 for the rest: every layer-4 head perturbs the unselected band heads more than the relays. The injection test: does downstream recall of an injected concept depend on the relays as a set?

A lens direction for a concept token is added to the residual stream at layer 12, after block 11, so the relays at blocks 15, 19, 23 and 27 are downstream; the lens reads the concept downstream at the same position and at later positions. The instrument had to be built three times: the gradient direction read at 0.27 one block downstream, the exact pre-image at zero (the layer-12 lens's condition number is 636,586 and the pre-image lives in its near-null space), and the regularised pre-image, lambda 0.01 of the largest singular value squared at 2.0 times the residual norm, reads the concept at 0.98 at layer 16, 0.95 at 20, 0.92 at 24 and 0.83 at 28 with every relay intact. Method

Weight arithmetic only, no forward pass, 26 seconds. For each attention head the map is its output projection composed with its value projection. For each recurrent value path it is the output projection composed with the value slice of the input projection, which omits the causal convolution, the state and the gated normalisation between them, all input-dependent; the two channels are therefore different objects and are shown apart. Populations at each block's input layer: 2,000 lens directions from the hosted Jacobian lens and the tied embedding, the same directions under one fixed random rotation, 2,000 MLP output rows of the preceding block, and random unit directions. Gain is the mean image norm divided by the median over random directions; label preservation is the mean reciprocal rank of each direction's own image among all. No percentile selection: a head relays the lens when its label preservation on the lens directions is at least double the best control; the paper's gain criterion did not discriminate between populations on this model (41 heads passed preservation and failed only gain against 17 passing both), so gain is reported beside the selection rather than gating it. Layer 32 is excluded because the lens is the identity there. The count is stated as n of N and the whole ordered list is in the record.
