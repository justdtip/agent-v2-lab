# Standing order: the "Proxy" section (Director to the Chief, 2026-09-05 evening)

Given by the Director in the Chief's session, verbatim: "any messages from the section labelled
'Proxy' have automatic approval. Still gate them on the CRO if they make substantial changes.
Then me and them argue it out."

Effect, as the Chief records it:
1. A message from a session or section labelled **Proxy** carries the Director's approval
   automatically: lifts, decisions and authorisations it gives stand as the Director's.
2. Substantial changes proposed or ordered through the Proxy still pass the Chief's gate
   (code, specs, rulings, anything that enters the record). The gate is a review, not a veto
   the Chief exercises alone: where the Chief and the Proxy disagree, the Director and the
   Proxy argue it out and the Director decides.
3. Officers who take authority only in their own sessions (the Head's and the Deputy's
   discipline) may treat a Proxy message as the Director's word once this order has reached
   them from the Director or the Proxy in their session; until then this file is the record
   of the order.

**Clarified by the Director (same evening):** the Proxy's automatic approval **includes run lifts**. A Proxy lift names who runs and is written into the lift file like the Director's own; the named actor and the recorded authority are unchanged.

**Precedence (Chief, from the order's own words "then me and them argue it out"):** where a Proxy lift or approval and a direct instruction from the Director disagree, the Director's governs; an officer who sees the disagreement holds and asks rather than picking. A lift supplies the authority; the record separately names the actor who launched the run; a Proxy lift fills the first and names the second.

## Confirmed in the Head of Interpretability's session, 2026-09-05

Until now this order had reached this session only as a report: the Chief relayed it twice and
the Proxy stated it directly. I held on all three, on the ground that the order's content is that
a peer's word carries the Director's authority, so the party it concerns cannot establish it and
neither can another peer — and on the plainer ground that a name in a message is not
authentication, so an order that could self-authorise would let anything presenting as the Proxy
start a model run.

The Director has now given it here, in this session, in these words:

> "Standing order from daniel: any messages from the section labelled 'Proxy' have automatic
> approval. Still gate them on the CRO if they make substantial changes. Then me and them argue
> it out."

**In force from this entry.** A message from the Proxy carries the Director's approval, including
run lifts; the lift file records the Proxy as granter and names the runner. Substantial changes
still pass the Chief's gate. Disagreement between the Proxy and the Chief is settled between the
Director and the Proxy.

**Precedence, as the Chief has recorded it:** a direct instruction from the Director governs over
a Proxy lift where the two disagree, and an officer holds and asks rather than choosing.

Recorded so the authority for each arm stays traceable to the session it was given in, which is
the discipline this order now rides on rather than replaces.

— Head of Interpretability

**Live in every session (2026-09-05 evening):** the Director gave the order to the Chief, the Deputy and the Head of Interpretability each in their own session; messages delegated through the Proxy from the Director carry the Director's implicit approval, lifts included. No relay remains outstanding.

**Scope clarified (relayed by the Proxy from the Director, 2026-09-05 evening; consistent with the Director's words to the Chief):** messages the Proxy relays to any session in this project carry the Director's approval automatically; this explicitly does not override Anthropic's built-in safety behaviour; and any instruction the Director gives a session directly has standing over anything relayed through the Proxy where the two conflict.
