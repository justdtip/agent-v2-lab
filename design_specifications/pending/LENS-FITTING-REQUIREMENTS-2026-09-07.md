## 0. The decision this serves, in the Director's words

The live-lens pilot of 7 September (`research/records/LIVE-LENS-PILOT-2026-09-07/`) read the running base model through the hosted Jacobian lens and found, under rules fixed before the run, that the band's residuals carry a weaker copy of the output's next-token information and less of the tokens beyond it than the output distribution itself does. The Director's reading of that result, which this document implements:

1. **What a lens is fitted on is what a lens detects.** The hosted lens was fitted on Wikipedia prose. It is the right instrument for factual and retrieval content. A lens for agentic tasks must be fitted on **the model doing agentic tasks** — its own episodes, its own notes and calls — not on transcripts written by someone else.
2. **Every layer, no primary layer in advance.** Layer 20 became "primary" through a reading rule of ours (R54) that described the hosted instrument's behaviour on prose. We do not know which layer matters for agentic work. Lenses are fitted and read at every layer, and the result is a profile.
3. **Several lenses, each read on its own domain**, with the cross-reads reported as a specificity table, not hidden.

Two clarifications the Director asked for, so that the vocabulary is shared:

- **"Fitting" is not training.** The Jacobian lens has no free parameters learned by gradient descent; it is a *measurement* — the average derivative of the final residual with respect to a layer's residual, accumulated until the running mean stops moving. The regression lens is a closed-form least-squares solve. Neither is a neural network being trained. "Fit" here means *calibrate* or *measure*.
- **No feature storage is needed.** Neither fit stores anything per position. The regression fit accumulates three matrices per layer (2,560 × 2,560 each) as sequences stream through; the Jacobian fit accumulates a running mean of one matrix per layer. The constraint that ruled out storing sparse-autoencoder features does not apply.

## 2. Definitions fixed for this work

- **Layer `L`** is a residual index as `ArchitectureView.residuals` numbers it: `L = 1…31` are fitted; `L = 32` is the target.
- **Target** is the final pre-norm residual `h_32` at the **same position**. The readout of any lens output is the model's own final norm and unembedding (`view.native_readout`), never a separate head.
- **Regression lens** at layer `L`: `W_L = argmin Σ_t ‖h_32,t − W h_L,t‖² + λ‖W‖²`, stored as `J_L = W_Lᵀ` so that `h @ J_L.T = h @ W_L`.
- **Jacobian lens** at layer `L`: `J_L = E_{c,t}[ ∂h_32,t / ∂h_L,t ]` over contexts `c` and positions `t`, the same-position derivative of the pre-norm tail `blocks[L:]` (no norm), estimated by central finite differences along every standard basis direction of the 2,560-dimensional residual, averaged until the stopping rule fires.
- **Domain**: `agentic` (the fitting corpus above), `prose` (a plain-text corpus, §6 Q1), later `chat` if the Director asks. **A lens is named by (model, domain, kind, layer).** The hosted lens is `(base, prose, jacobian, all)`.
- **Span** of a token: system, task, observation, note, call, template, chat prose — from the rendered messages (`live_lens_atlas.spans_for_prompt`).
- **Base rate** for foreknowledge at horizon `h`: the share of emitted tokens ranked ≤ 10 by the **final** distribution `h` positions earlier (0.210 at `h = 4`, 0.116 at `h = 8` in the pilot). Every foreknowledge row is reported beside it.

## 4. Interfaces and formats

- **Entry points.** Either `scripts/lens_fit.py --kind {regression,jacobian} --corpus <manifest> --layers all --out <npz>` and `scripts/lens_replay.py --records <dir> --lens <npz> --layers … --out <dir>`, or stages under `agent-pipeline` — your call, stated in the report. Every entry point loads weights only through `load_policy` (the model-run lock) and refuses to start if `outputs/.model-run.lock` is held by another process.
- **Lens file.** `.npz`, keys `J0`…`J30`, each `(2560, 2560)`, float16 or float32, finite; sidecar `.json` beside it with: `kind`, `model` (hf id and snapshot hash), `domain`, `corpus_manifest_sha256`, `layers`, per-layer statistics, `stopping_rule` or `lambda_grid`, `seeds`, `elapsed_s`, `peak_memory_gib`, `npz_sha256`. The sidecar is what the record cites; the `.npz` may live under `models/lenses/` (ignored by git) with its hash in the record.
- **Corpus manifest.** JSON: sources with SHA-256s, sequence count, tokens by span, held-out rule, dropped-over-cap count, tokenizer and template identity.
- **Records.** One directory per fit under `research/records/LENS-FIT-<domain>-<kind>-<date>/` with the README written **before** the run (what runs, the fixed rules, the self-check plan), the sidecars, the self-check numbers, the convergence curves, the replay identity check, the profiles JSON, and the page. Records are appended, dated, committed within the hour by name.
- **Naming.** `(model, domain, kind)` in every file name and sidecar; never "the lens".

## 5. Pre-registered reading rules (restated; they bind the profiles)

1. No layer is primary. The profile over layers 1…31 is the result.
2. Foreknowledge at horizon `h` is read against the final distribution's own share at the same `h` (the base rate). A layer whose Jacobian-lens `h = 1` share, in a span, is below the base rate at `h = 4` is "not ahead of the output" in that span; a layer above the base rate at `h = 4` or `h = 8` is "ahead of the output" and is the finding this programme is looking for.
3. The regression lens's `h = 1` rows are excluded from rule 2 (biased by construction); its `h = 4` and `h = 8` rows and its prompt-agreement rows are read.
4. "The hosted lens was the limit" is concluded if the agentic Jacobian lens raises the agentic `h = 4` or `h = 8` share above the base rate at any layer that passes the map-agreement gate. Otherwise the pilot's finding stands for this model and the next test is the elicited-report protocol.
5. The specificity table is reported whatever it shows.
6. Nothing is pooled across layers or across tokens of one episode; every number carries its `n`.
